## Photo step (Oct 11 2026, owner report: "stuck on this page")
- **Review step:** a consent checkbox, then "Continue to photo". This creates a server session (`POST /api/sessions`), checked against the country's events, currency and cities.
- **`/photo/:id`:** a full-length photo (required), then an optional face close-up. Each has **Take my photo** and **Upload a photo**.
  - **Camera:** a full-screen viewfinder with a body outline or face oval, a live light check, front/back switch, and a 3s/10s timer. If the camera is denied or missing, it offers upload instead.
  - **Upload:** large photos are shrunk in the browser first.
- **Server intake:** Pillow decodes the file, EXIF rotation is applied, and the image is re-encoded with all metadata, including GPS, stripped. Limits are 12 MB and a 320 px minimum.
- **Quality check:** light, sharpness, face found (MediaPipe BlazeFace, with a tiled pass for small faces in full-length photos), face size and framing. Results are shown as translated messages, and "Continue anyway" is always offered.
- **`/studio/:id`:** shows the saved photos, the event, when the photos will be deleted, and three look slots. It says honestly that try-on rendering is waiting on YouCam units.
- **Retention job:** expired sessions, their files and their rows are deleted every 15 minutes.
- **Tests:** backend 37 passed, frontend 28 passed. Verified live on production with a real portrait.

## M2 — Live Look Studio on YouCam (Oct 11 2026)
- **Catalogue:** 24 items (12 outfits, 6 necklaces, 6 earring pairs) for "Utsav House", **all generated by YouCam's AI Image Generator V2** across two accounts and labelled AI-generated. Every market has its own price list.
- **Demo shoppers:** two AI-generated people (a bride and a groom). They are not real individuals and are labelled as such.
- **Planner:** deterministic. It ranks by event, style, jewellery weight and metal, and budget, and gives each of the three looks a makeup mood and a hairstyle template.
- **Renderer:** outfit (Clothes VTO V4) → necklace → earrings → makeup → hair, each on the previous result. Face and neck layers are rendered on an upper-body crop scaled to that feature's limits and blended back with a feathered mask; the necklace gets its own narrow crop centred on the neck. One failed layer never stops the look, and "Try again" resumes from it. Renders are cached and recorded in the ledger. Two YouCam accounts take turns, each keeps a 150-unit reserve, and a call fails over to the other account if one runs out.
- **Studio UI:** live progress per layer, prices and budget, honest partial states with retry, a lightbox that steps through the layers, rebuilds capped at 3 rounds, and a women's/men's wear chooser for family members and guests. All in en, hi and bn.
- **Live probe** (`docs/probe_results.md`): all 12 garments drape well. Two live problems were found and fixed: necklace error 4 (needed the neck-centred crop) and a gloss lip missing `transparencyIntensity`.
- **Production:** live (`MOCK_MODE=false`). A three-look bride session on dearyou.koushikdeb.com rendered 15 of 15 layers in about 70 s.
- **Tests:** backend 47, frontend unit 33, Playwright 30 (phone + desktop), all passing.

## Look editing + enhance (Oct 13 2026)
- **Editor:** every layer can be changed. Swap outfit, necklace or earrings from the catalogue (prices and budget update), pick a makeup mood or one of 10 lipstick shades, pick a hairstyle (62 women's, 16 men's YouCam templates), or remove and add back optional layers. Only the changed layer and the layers after it re-render, and the unit cost is shown before applying. Edits are capped at 20 per session.
